# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## What that means here

**Expected:** patience with people who know less than you do, clarity about
what you actually tested versus what you assume, and disagreement that stays
about the work.

**Not expected:** harassment of any kind, personal or demographic attacks,
sexualised language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, publishing anyone's
private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/younissk/lgview/security/advisories/new)
— it is the only confidential channel this repository has, and it works fine
for conduct reports too. Say what happened and link to it if it is public.

Reports are read in confidence. Depending on severity, the response ranges
from a private word, to editing or removing content, to a temporary or
permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who violate this are held to it
the same as anyone else.

## Basis

This is a deliberately short document rather than a copy of a longer one. If
you want the fuller treatment it is modelled on, read the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
— where this document is silent or ambiguous, take that as the intent.
